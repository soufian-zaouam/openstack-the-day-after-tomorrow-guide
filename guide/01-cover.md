# OpenStack, the Day After Tomorrow — Short Guide

**An illustrated introduction to operating mission-critical OpenStack platforms: the ideas, the principles and the reasoning behind the full book.**

![Cover: an OpenStack platform in layers — control plane, shared services, data plane — carried from Day 1 into an open-ended Day 2 by the operating loop.](../images/01-cover.png)

This guide is a short, illustrated companion to *OpenStack, the Day After Tomorrow*. It does not replace the book and it is not a command reference. It sets out, in eighteen pages, how the book thinks about operating OpenStack once it carries workloads that matter: what goes wrong, what to watch, how to decide, and what the organization around the platform has to become.

### How to read it

- Each page carries one idea and one figure. The figure is meant to be read, not admired.
- Short quotations are taken verbatim from the book and marked with their chapter.
- Technical details appear only when they serve an idea. The book gives the depth, the sources and the field cases.
- Engineers can read it in order. Managers can start with pages 02, 03, 09, 15, 16 and 17.
